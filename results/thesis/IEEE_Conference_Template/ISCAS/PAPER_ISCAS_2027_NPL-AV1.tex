\begin{abstract}
The intra-frame coding of the AOMedia Video 1 (AV1) codec selects the block
partitioning through a recursive rate-distortion search, and its reference
encoder already embeds a convolutional neural network that prunes this search at
the faster speed presets. Fast partitioning solutions are usually compared
against whole presets, which reconfigure many unrelated coding stages, so the
merit of a learned pruner itself remains unmeasured. This paper presents the
Neural Pruner Ladder for AV1 (NPL-AV1), a set of lightweight neural networks that
replace the native network as drop-in partition pruners at the same speed preset.
A pre-search pruner decides how much of the partition tree is opened before any
rate-distortion evaluation, and a post-NONE pruner decides whether the search
continues after the unpartitioned node is measured, a decision point that the
native encoder leaves unused in All-Intra coding. Experimental results over the
eight UHD 4K sequences of the AOM Common Test Conditions show that the post-NONE
pruner achieves time savings of 30.34\% with a Bjontegaard delta bitrate (BD-BR)
of 0.41\% at preset 1, statistically on par with the native network, and lowers
its BD-BR significantly at high quality at presets 1 and 2, at a cost of losing
at preset 3. The pre-search pruner extends the time savings of each preset up to
77.30\%, at a BD-BR of 4.35\%. Twelve of the fifteen nondominated operating points
measured belong to NPL-AV1. % [completar: frase do H9d empilhado, apos a campanha D2]
To the best of the authors' knowledge, this is the first solution in the
literature measured as a drop-in replacement of the native partition network of
AV1.
\end{abstract}
